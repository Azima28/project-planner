# Planning Completeness Checklist

Use sections triggered by archetype/capability; record N/A explicitly.

- Outcome, scope, owner, audience, operating mode, and constraints have source/evidence.
- Every active requirement/work item has acceptance criteria and graph origin.
- Relevant delivery has failure, recovery, accessibility/usability, test, and operational ownership.
- Relevant data has provenance, classification, access, retention/deletion, and migration/rollback.
- Relevant integration has access status, fallback, owner, evidence, and release condition.
- Relevant money/regulated/safety capability has non-waivable boundary and authoritative review.
- Artifacts include/skip are justified; validated files have matching metadata/hash.
- Reports show gaps and eligibility honestly.
